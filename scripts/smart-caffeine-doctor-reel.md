# Smart Caffeine – Doctor Reel Scripts (Hinglish)

Three versions to shoot on the same day and post as separate reels.
Whichever performs best decides what to make next.

**Before shooting**
- Real moments only: times, chai counts and emergencies must be true for her.
- Keep patients and patient records out of frame; get permission to film in wards/corridors.
- End every version with: *Personal experience. Not medical advice.*
- Confirm the brand approves "alert, not jittery" and the L-theanine mention.

---

## Hook options

1. "12 ghante ki shift. 3 hacks jo mujhe raat 2 baje bhi sharp rakhte hain."
2. "Doctor hoon, aur maine shift mein chai chhod di. Here's why."
3. "Canteen tak jaana: 10 minute. Ye: 10 second."
4. "Raat ke 2 baje, 3rd emergency. Aur body bol rahi hai: bas."
5. "Pehle 4 chai pe chalti thi. Ab ek sachet pe."
6. "Night duty karne wale, ye video save kar lo."
7. "Wo ek mistake jo main har shift mein karti thi."
8. "Doctor ki coat pocket mein kya hota hai? Stethoscope, pen… aur ye."
9. "Chai ka crash OPD mein afford nahi hota."
10. "Mera 4 PM slump fix karne ka 10-second tareeka."
11. "Main caffeine ko lekar doctor ki tarah sochti hoon. Matlab timing sab kuch hai."
12. "12-hour shift survival kit: sirf 3 cheezein."
